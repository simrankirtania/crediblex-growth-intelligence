How this works: every score on this dashboard (SME Opportunity Score, Recommended Product, Partner Score, Early Warning Status) is computed deterministically in python/opportunity_engine.ipynb and mirrored in sql/analysis.sql. The AI layer (python/ai_insights.ipynb) is prompted — see ai/prompts.md — to explain those pre-computed results in business language, not to generate its own figures. This dashboard reproduces the same template-based explanation logic client-side so every SME and partner record has a brief on click.
